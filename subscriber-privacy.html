SUBSCRIBER INFORMATION

Privacy & Subscriber Terms

Pre-launch draft: this policy will be finalized before registration opens.

What information we request

When registration launches, we plan to collect your name, email address, and optional phone number, plus your verification status, consent choices, and date and method of signup.

How we'll use it

With your permission, The Hommeyosh may send announcements about new videos, game development builds, exclusive content links, and occasional promotional offers. Email marketing and SMS marketing will require separate choices. Supplying a phone number will be optional.

Text message consent

If you explicitly opt in to promotional SMS, message frequency will vary. Message and data rates may apply. Consent is not required for a purchase or an email subscription. Reply STOP to opt out and HELP for assistance once SMS is activated.

Verification and access

We intend to verify email addresses and optional phone numbers using six-digit codes. Subscriber details will be stored in a private database accessible only to authorized administrators and necessary service providers.

Unsubscribing

Subscribers will be able to stop marketing emails and text messages independently. Functional unsubscribe and contact mechanisms must be implemented before subscriptions open.

Provider details and contact

Our final policy will identify relevant service providers, a support contact, and appropriate data retention details before we collect subscriber information.
