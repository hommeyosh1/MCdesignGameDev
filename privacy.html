Privacy & Subscription Terms

Effective October 18, 2026 — Draft for review before launch.

What we collect

If you choose to subscribe, we collect your email address, optional phone number, verification status, consent choices, signup time, and a record of your subscription preferences. We use this information to send your requested creator announcements and manage opt-outs.

Messages you can choose

Email updates may include videos, upcoming releases, exclusive content links, and game development announcements. Promotional SMS is optional and requires a separate checkbox. Frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. No purchase is required.

Verification

We send one-time six-digit codes to confirm contact information. Verification codes expire. We do not sell subscriber lists.

Unsubscribe

Promotional texts: reply STOP to opt out, or HELP for assistance. Email: reply to a message asking to unsubscribe until the automatic unsubscribe link is added. We will respect opt-out requests.

Providers and retention

Hosting, database, email, and SMS providers may process information to deliver the service. Subscription and consent records are retained while needed to manage preferences and comply with applicable requirements.

Contact

Contact information and an automated preference link will be published before accepting real subscriptions.
